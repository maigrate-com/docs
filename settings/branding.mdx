The Branding settings page lets you replace the default Maigrate logo in the navigation sidebar with your organization's logo.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-branding.png" alt="Branding settings showing the current logo preview and logo upload control" />
</Frame>

Navigate to **Settings → Branding** to access this page.

## Uploading a logo

1. Click **Upload Logo** on the Branding settings page
2. Select an image file (SVG or PNG recommended; max 2 MB)
3. Click **Save**

The logo appears in the top-left corner of the sidebar, replacing the default Maigrate mark.

## Logo recommendations

- **Format**: SVG is ideal — it scales cleanly at any size. PNG works too.
- **Dimensions**: The sidebar logo area is approximately 120 × 40 px. A wide/horizontal logo or wordmark works best.
- **Background**: The sidebar has a light background. Use a logo with a transparent background or one designed for light backgrounds.
- **Size**: Keep the file under 2 MB.

## Reverting to the default logo

To remove your custom logo and revert to the Maigrate mark, click **Remove Logo** on the Branding settings page.
