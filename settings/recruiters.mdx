The Recruiters settings page manages the human team members in Maigrate AI Hub. Each recruiter profile stores contact information and links to their Google Calendar for interview scheduling.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-recruiters.png" alt="Recruiters settings showing recruiter list with timezone, status, and calendar links" />
</Frame>

Navigate to **Settings → Recruiters** to access this page.

## Adding a recruiter

Click **Add Recruiter** and fill in:
- **First and last name**
- **Email address** — must match the recruiter's Google Workspace account (used for calendar access)
- **Timezone** — used to interpret their calendar availability and calculate interview slot times

<Note>
  Recruiters can also be auto-created during candidate intake. If the assigned recruiter on an incoming application doesn't match an existing recruiter profile in Maigrate, one is created automatically from the intake data.
</Note>

## Connecting Google Calendar

Each recruiter needs a connected Google Calendar for the scheduling agent to read their availability and book interviews.

1. Go to **Settings → Recruiters** and click on a recruiter's name
2. Click **Connect Calendar** or navigate to the recruiter's calendar page
3. The calendar page shows the recruiter's current availability blocks pulled from Google Calendar

For this to work, your Google Workspace administrator must complete the domain-wide delegation setup. See [Google Workspace Integration](/integrations/google-workspace).

## Setting business hours and availability

Business hours tell the scheduling agent **which times of day it's allowed to offer** for a recruiter's interviews. You can set one default window for the whole week, then override or close individual days.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-recruiter-business-hours.png" alt="Edit Recruiter panel showing Timezone, Business Hours Start and End, and the Day-specific business hours editor with per-day Start, End, and Off controls" />
</Frame>

To edit a recruiter's hours:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recruiter's editor">
    Go to **Settings → Recruiters**, find the recruiter in the list, and click **Edit** under **Actions**. The **Edit Recruiter** panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the timezone">
    Check the **Timezone** field (for example, `America/New_York`). All the hours below are interpreted in this timezone, so slot times come out correct for the recruiter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default business hours">
    Use **Business Hours Start** and **Business Hours End** to set the window that applies every day by default (for example, `09:00 AM` to `05:00 PM`). Click the time field to type or pick a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Override specific days (optional)">
    Expand **Day-specific business hours** to fine-tune individual days. See the table below for what each control does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update** to save. The scheduling agent uses the new hours the next time it looks for slots.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Day-specific business hours

Expanding **Day-specific business hours** shows a row for each day of the week, each with its own controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start** | The earliest time the agent will offer on that day. Setting it overrides the default Business Hours Start, just for that day. |
| **End** | The latest time the agent will offer on that day. Setting it overrides the default Business Hours End, just for that day. |
| **Off** | Closes that day entirely. When **Off** is checked, the agent offers **no** interview slots on that weekday and the Start/End fields are disabled. |

Common examples:

- **A recruiter who finishes early on Fridays** — leave the default at 9–5, then set **Friday** End to `12:00 PM`.
- **A recruiter who doesn't work Mondays** — check **Off** on **Monday**.
- **Different hours midweek** — set **Wednesday** Start to `10:30 AM` and End to `07:00 PM`.

Any day you don't change keeps the default Business Hours window.

<Note>
  Business hours and Google Calendar work together. Business hours set the *outer window* the agent is allowed to offer; the recruiter's Google Calendar busy blocks then remove any times that are already booked. A day marked **Off** is closed regardless of what the calendar shows.
</Note>

## Viewing calendar availability

Click on a recruiter's name, then **View Calendar**. The calendar page shows their free/busy blocks for the upcoming days — the same view the scheduling agent uses when generating interview slots.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/recruiter-calendar.png" alt="Recruiter calendar view showing free and busy blocks for the upcoming days" />
</Frame>

If the calendar shows no availability when you expect slots, check:
- The recruiter's Google Calendar has events blocking the time (the agent treats busy blocks as unavailable)
- The domain-wide delegation is set up correctly
- The recruiter's email in Maigrate matches their Google Workspace email exactly

## Recruiter assignment

Candidates are assigned to recruiters based on the assignment data in the incoming application. You can manually change the assignment from the Details tab in any candidate's application drawer.

If a candidate doesn't have an assigned recruiter when scheduling is triggered, the scheduling agent will pick one automatically based on availability.
