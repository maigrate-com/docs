---
title: "Block Candidates"
description: "Configure rules for automatically blocking specific candidates from being processed."
---

The Block Candidates settings page lets you define rules that prevent certain candidates from moving through the automated pipeline.

Navigate to **Settings → Block Candidates** to access this page.

## What blocking does

When a candidate matches a block rule, they are excluded from automated screening and scheduling. They will not receive automated outreach. The application is not deleted — it remains visible in the Applicants board, but automation does not proceed.

## Common use cases

- **Previous no-hire decisions**: Block candidates who have been rejected before from re-entering the pipeline
- **Active disqualifications**: Block candidates flagged by HR or legal from receiving outreach
- **Domain or email blocks**: Block all applications from a specific email domain

## Rule types

Block rules can match on:
- Email address (exact match)
- Phone number (exact match)
- Email domain (matches all addresses at that domain)
- Candidate ID

## Reviewing blocked candidates

Blocked candidates appear in the Applicants board with a blocked status indicator. You can remove a block rule from this settings page to un-block a candidate and allow automation to proceed.

<Warning>
  Removing a block rule does not automatically re-trigger automation for previously blocked candidates. You'll need to manually trigger screening or scheduling from the candidate's Details tab.
</Warning>
