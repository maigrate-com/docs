The Screening settings page lets you write organization-level instructions that the screening agent applies to every candidate evaluation.

Navigate to **Settings → Screening** to access this page.

## Screening directives

Screening directives are plain-language instructions that supplement the job description when the agent evaluates a candidate. They're your opportunity to encode your organization's hiring standards directly into the screening logic.

### What to put in directives

Directives work best for:
- **Minimum requirements** — "Candidates must have at least 2 years of relevant experience"
- **Disqualifying factors** — "Reject candidates who have not completed required certifications"
- **Soft criteria** — "Prefer candidates located within commuting distance of the job site"
- **Context** — "This role requires rotating shift availability; candidates who specify fixed hours only should be marked for review"

Directives should be factual and specific. Avoid vague instructions like "look for good candidates" — the agent evaluates against concrete criteria more reliably.

### What directives don't do

Directives can't override the agent's baseline rules:
- The agent will not invent facts about a candidate to justify a decision
- When evidence is ambiguous, the agent prefers `Review` over `Reject`
- Directives are organization-wide — they apply to all roles unless your directives themselves include role-specific conditions

### Saving directives

Type your directives in the text area and click **Save**. The new directives take effect on the next screening run. Existing decisions are not retroactively changed.

## Re-running screening

After updating your directives, you may want to re-screen candidates whose decisions were influenced by the old criteria. Use the **Run Screening** button in each candidate's Details tab to trigger a fresh evaluation.

<Note>
  Re-running screening overwrites the previous agent decision with the new one. Recruiter overrides are not affected — they remain the authoritative decision.
</Note>

## Recruiter overrides

Recruiters can always override the agent's screening decision on a per-candidate basis from the Screening tab in the application drawer. Overrides are stored separately from the agent's decision and take precedence for all downstream actions (scheduling, Salesforce writeback, etc.).

See [Screening](/recruiter/screening) for the override workflow.
