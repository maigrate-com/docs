The Scheduling settings page controls how the scheduling agent finds and proposes interview slots, and how aggressively it follows up with candidates.

<Frame>

Navigate to **Settings → Scheduling** to access this page.

## Scheduling guidance

Scheduling guidance is free-text context the agent uses when making decisions about interview scheduling. Use it for:
- Special availability constraints ("Our office is closed on Wednesdays")
- Preferred slot times ("Morning slots are preferred for tech roles")
- Recruiter-specific notes ("Recruiter Sarah only does video interviews")

This is different from recruiter calendar configuration — guidance affects how the agent interprets availability, while calendar access determines what slots are actually free.

## Default slot rules

| Rule | Default value |
|---|---|
| Slot duration | 30 minutes |
| Initial slot count | 3 proposed slots |
| Search window | Next 7 days |
| Recruiter business hours | 9 AM – 5 PM (recruiter's timezone) |
| Weekend slots | Disabled by default |
| After-hours slots | Disabled by default |
| Max interviews per recruiter per day | Configurable per organization (no default cap) |

Weekend and after-hours slot requests from candidates are escalated to a human rather than rejected outright — the recruiter decides whether to make an exception.

## Follow-up timing (default policy)

The default follow-up policy ships with 4 follow-up attempts (in addition to the initial outreach, so 5 total candidate touches):

| Attempt | Default delay | Channel | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st follow-up | 5 hours after initial outreach | SMS | Direct CTA |
| 2nd follow-up | 5 hours after attempt 1 | Email | Structured email |
| 3rd follow-up | 1 business day after attempt 2 (morning) | SMS | Casual nudge |
| 4th follow-up | 2 business days after attempt 3 | Email | Soft close (marks unresponsive after sending) |

These defaults are encoded in a plain-language policy that's editable in the UI — the Preview Rules button compiles your edited policy into the executable schedule. The number of attempts, the delay between each, the channel, and the time-of-day preference (immediate vs morning) are all configurable. Defaults: send between **8 AM and 7 PM** candidate-local; weekends are skipped.

After the maximum attempts are exhausted, the thread's follow-up state is marked `unresponsive`. The application's scheduling status moves to `failed_outreach`, which surfaces it in the Failed Outreach view on the Applicants board.

## Send window

Two send windows apply:

- **Outbound SMS** (any sender): 8 AM – 9 PM in the contact's local time. This is a global channel-routing rule that applies to every SMS we send, regardless of which agent or template.
- **Follow-up sends specifically**: 8 AM – 7 PM in the candidate's local time, set by the follow-up policy.

If a message would be sent outside the applicable window, it's queued until the window opens.

## Recruiter calendar configuration

Recruiter calendar access is configured per-recruiter in [Settings → Recruiters](/settings/recruiters). Each recruiter must have a Google Calendar connected for scheduling to work.
