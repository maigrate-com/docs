The Document Review settings page controls the policy the document check agent uses when extracting and validating candidate credentials.

Navigate to **Settings → Document Review** to access this page.

## What document review does

When a candidate submits a document (resume, certification, license), the document check agent:
1. Extracts the relevant information from the document
2. Validates it against your configured policy (e.g., checks expiration dates, required credential types)
3. Returns a pass, fail, or escalation for human review

## Credential review policy

The policy defines what credentials are required, what constitutes a valid credential, and what should trigger an escalation vs. an automatic rejection.

Write your policy in plain language. Examples:
- "Candidates must have a current, valid certification in X. Expired certifications should be flagged for review."
- "Resumes must show at least 18 months of continuous relevant experience."
- "Any gap in employment longer than 6 months should be noted in the rationale."

## When document review escalates

The document check agent escalates when:
- A required document is missing or unreadable
- A credential is expired and the policy doesn't specify automatic rejection
- The extracted information is ambiguous or contradictory
- The document format is unexpected and extraction confidence is low

Escalations from document review appear in the Command Center like any other escalation.

## Document storage

Candidate documents are stored securely and accessible to recruiters. Download the original document from the Details tab in the application drawer.
