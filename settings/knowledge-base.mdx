title: "Knowledge Base"

---

The Knowledge Base is a store of public-facing information about your organization that the scheduling and communication agents can reference when candidates ask questions.

Navigate to **Settings → Knowledge Base** to access this page.

## What the knowledge base is for

Candidates often ask basic questions during scheduling conversations:
- "What does this role involve?"
- "Where is the office located?"
- "Is there parking on site?"
- "Is this role remote or on-site?"
- "What's the pay range?"

Rather than escalating every question to a recruiter, the agent can answer using the knowledge base — if the answer is there. If it's not, the agent acknowledges it doesn't have the information and offers to have a recruiter follow up.

## What to include

Good knowledge base content is factual, public-facing, and relevant to candidates at the interview stage:

- Office locations and directions
- Parking and transit information
- Remote/hybrid/on-site policy
- Benefits overview
- Interview format (video, in-person, panel)
- Dress code
- What to bring to the interview
- Company mission and culture (brief)

## What not to include

- Confidential compensation details (unless you want the agent to share them)
- Internal process notes meant for recruiters
- Role-specific details that change between jobs (put those in the job description in your ATS)

## Agent behavior with the knowledge base

The agent is instructed not to invent facts. If a candidate asks something not covered in the knowledge base, the agent says so honestly — it won't fabricate an answer. When updating the knowledge base, be specific: vague entries lead to vague agent answers.

Changes to the knowledge base take effect immediately on new conversations. Existing in-flight conversations may take a few messages to reflect the update.
