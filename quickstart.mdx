## 1. Log in

Navigate to your Maigrate Hub URL and enter your work email address. You'll be redirected to your organization's login page. After authenticating, you'll land on the **Command Center**.

<Note>
  If you see a blank screen or an error after login, your IT admin may need to provision your account. Contact your Maigrate administrator.
</Note>

## 2. Orient yourself in the Command Center

The Command Center is your default home. It shows three panels:

- **Escalations** — items that need a human decision before the agents can continue
- **Upcoming Interviews** — interviews scheduled in the next 48 hours
- **Review Queue** — candidates whose screening decision needs your sign-off

If this is a fresh deployment, the panels will be empty. Candidates appear here as they enter the system.

## 3. Explore the Applicants board

Click **Applicants** in the left sidebar. This is your full application board — every candidate the system has processed.

Click any row to open the **application drawer**. The drawer has three tabs:

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Contact info, recruiter assignment, resume download, manual action buttons |
| **Screening** | AI decision, rationale, candidate evidence, and override controls |
| **Communication** | Full message thread with the candidate, direct message input, and operator guidance |

## 4. Review a screening decision

Open any candidate in the **Screening** tab. You'll see:

- The **decision** (`Advance`, `Review`, or `Reject`) with a confidence indicator
- The agent's **written rationale** explaining why
- The **candidate evidence** (application answers, job context) the agent used

If you agree, no action is needed. To override:

1. Select the correct decision from the dropdown
2. Add a brief reason (required for overrides)
3. Click **Save Override**

Your override becomes the authoritative decision for all downstream steps.

## 5. Resolve your first escalation

Head back to the **Command Center**. Click any escalation to open it.

Each escalation shows:
- **What happened** — the agent's explanation of why it stopped
- **Candidate context** — who this is and where they are in the funnel
- **Resolution options** — typically resolve, send a message, or dismiss the agent

After you take action, click **Mark Resolved**. The escalation clears from the queue and (depending on your configuration) the agent resumes automatically.

## 6. Send a direct message

In the **Communication** tab of any application drawer, you can message the candidate directly. Type your message, select SMS or Email, and click **Send**. The message is delivered via your configured Twilio/Resend account and logged in the thread.

You can also send **operator guidance** — a private note visible only to the agent, not the candidate. Use this to give the agent context or redirect its next action.
