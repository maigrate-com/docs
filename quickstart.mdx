Most of the work in Maigrate AI Hub happens without you. Candidates apply, get screened, get messaged, and book their own interviews — all on autopilot. Your day is the short list of things the agents can't do alone.

That comes down to three things:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your agenda">
    See what's on today — interviews, and anything waiting on you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear your escalations">
    Handle the cases the agents flagged for a human decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record what happened">
    Mark interviews completed or no-show so the agents keep moving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you need more context on a candidate along the way, you open their record. That's the whole job. Everything below walks through each step.

<Note>
  **If you only have ten minutes, work the escalations.** Everything else is the agents running normally — escalations are the cases waiting on you, often with a clock running.
</Note>

## 1. Check your agenda

Start your day in the [**Interview Agenda**](/recruiter/agenda) — the clearest view of what's on. It lists every interview booked for the day you pick, in order down the day, with each one's status, the assigned recruiter, the candidate's reminder state, and the latest message exchanged. A summary line up top totals the day: completed, no-shows, canceled, pending, and still awaiting an outcome.

The agenda opens on **today** by default. Use the date picker (or **‹ Prev** / **Today** / **Next ›**) to look ahead or back, and filter by recruiter to see just one person's day. Unlike a simple upcoming feed, it also shows interviews that have already happened and how they turned out — so nothing slips through the cracks. It's where you'll record outcomes in step 3, too, without leaving the page.

Your other landing spot is the **Command Center**, which holds the rest of your work queue — escalations and the review queue. That's your next stop.

## 2. Clear your escalations

An **escalation** is the agent raising its hand. When it hits something it can't resolve on its own — an ambiguous reply, a calendar conflict, a question it isn't allowed to answer, a repeat no-show — it pauses automation on that candidate and surfaces the case in the **Command Center**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/command-center.png" alt="Command Center showing escalations, upcoming interviews, and the review queue" />
</Frame>

Click any escalation to open the candidate's record. You'll see what the agent did, why it stopped, and what it recommends. From there you usually do one of:

- **Send operator guidance** — a private instruction to the agent on how to proceed (e.g. *"Offer them next Monday at 9 AM"* or *"This candidate is exempt from the weekend policy — proceed with Saturday slots"*). The candidate never sees it.
- **Message the candidate directly** — take the reply yourself from the Communication tab.
- **Override the screening decision** — if it's screening-related, set it to `Advance`, `Review`, or `Reject` on the Screening tab.

Then click **Mark Resolved**. Each escalation type has a specific best move — the [Escalations guide](/recruiter/escalations) lists every reason code and exactly what to do for each.

<Note>
  Resolving an escalation doesn't always restart the agent on its own. If you want it to keep going, leave operator guidance telling it what to do next.
</Note>

## 3. Record what happened

After an interview's scheduled time passes, mark the outcome so the pipeline keeps moving. The fastest way is right on the [Interview Agenda](/recruiter/agenda): on any interview, open the menu next to its status and choose **Mark Completed** or **Mark No-Show**.

Marking a no-show automatically kicks the scheduling agent into rebooking — you don't have to chase the reschedule yourself.

<Note>
  Recording outcomes promptly is what keeps the agents working. An interview left un-marked stalls everything downstream for that candidate.
</Note>

## When you need more: dig into a candidate

Click any candidate — from the Command Center, the Agenda, or the [**Applicants**](/recruiter/applicants) board — to open their **application drawer**. This is the full record, organized into three tabs:

| Tab | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Contact info, recruiter assignment, resume, and manual actions (run screening, schedule, dismiss the agent) |
| **Screening** | The AI decision, its written rationale, the evidence it used, and override controls |
| **Communication** | The full message thread with the candidate, plus inputs to message them directly or send operator guidance |

The **Applicants** board is also where you go to find anyone who isn't on today's agenda — search or filter by decision, status, recruiter, or date.

## What you don't have to do

- **You don't run follow-ups.** Candidates who go quiet get nudged automatically on a set cadence. See [Follow-up policy](/recruiter/follow-up-policy).
- **You don't send interview reminders.** A same-day reminder fires on its own.
- **You don't update the ATS or Salesforce.** Maigrate writes back the events that matter.
