---
title: "Communicating With a Candidate"
description: "Direct messages vs operator guidance, when to dismiss the agent, and how to give guidance that actually works."
---

Every candidate-facing conversation in Maigrate lives in a thread the communication agent manages. When you intervene, you can do it in two very different ways — and getting them mixed up is the most common source of "wait, did I just send that to the candidate?" moments.

## The one distinction that matters

| Action | Who sees it | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct message** | The candidate | You want to say something specific to the candidate yourself. The agent will see your message and adapt its next turn. |
| **Operator guidance** | The agent (private) | You want to change what the agent does next without writing to the candidate. The agent treats your guidance as authoritative. |

The Communication tab keeps these in separate inputs. Confirm the selector before sending. For the full mechanics, see [Communication → Two actions, don't confuse them](/recruiter/communication#two-actions-dont-confuse-them).

## Good operator guidance

Operator guidance works best when it's specific and constraint-shaped:

- "Don't propose Friday slots — this recruiter is OOO."
- "Confirm the rate of $34/hr if the candidate asks."
- "This candidate is a referral. Prioritize accommodating their schedule."
- "Skip the next follow-up — I'm calling them directly."

Vague guidance ("be nice", "use your judgment") is harder for the agent to act on. Treat each piece of guidance as you would brief a junior teammate.

## When to dismiss instead of guide

Some situations shouldn't have an agent in them at all. Use **Dismiss Agent** when:

- The conversation is sensitive (complaints, accommodations, sensitive personal context).
- The exchange will be a long, contextual back-and-forth — compensation negotiation, ATS reconciliation.
- You've decided the agent's policy doesn't fit and you want full manual control.

Use operator guidance when the agent is mostly doing the right thing and needs **one constraint or fact** to keep going.

Once dismissed, a thread is `human_owned` and the agent will not act on it until you reset it. See [Communication → Dismissing the agent](/recruiter/communication#dismissing-the-agent).
