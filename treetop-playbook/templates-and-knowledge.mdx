---
title: "Editing Templates and Knowledge"
description: "When to change what the agents say to candidates, and what facts are safe to give them."
---

Two settings change agent output across every candidate going forward:

1. **Communication templates** — the wording of automated messages.
2. **Organization knowledge** — the public company / careers context the agents may reference.

Both apply to **future** messages, immediately. Past messages are unchanged.

## When to edit a template

| Family | Edit when |
|---|---|
| Initial Outreach | The first message to a candidate isn't getting enough engagement, or doesn't match your brand voice. |
| Follow-Up Nudges | Candidates aren't responding and your follow-ups feel generic — or they're too pushy. |
| Interview Confirmations | The post-booking confirmation is missing useful information (joining instructions, contact info). |
| Same-Day Reminders | The reminder text doesn't match your brand or is missing a piece of info. |

Edit in [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication). Keep variables intact — `{{candidate_name}}`, `{{available_slots}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}` — or messages will go out with literal placeholders.

The agent uses templates as a starting point and may adapt to context. Templates that are too rigid become awkward when the candidate asks a question or shifts the conversation. Aim for templates the agent can naturally extend.

## When to edit organization knowledge

[Settings → Knowledge Base](/settings/knowledge-base) is where you store public, candidate-safe facts about your company: how the role works, where you operate, what benefits look like, links to careers FAQ. The agents may reference this to answer candidate questions.

The agents are **explicitly instructed not to invent missing facts.** If the candidate asks something not in the knowledge base, the agent escalates instead of guessing. So:

- **Add what you'd be comfortable seeing quoted to a candidate verbatim.** That's the test.
- **Don't add confidential or candidate-sensitive info.** This includes compensation specifics by role, hiring volume, internal team org charts.
- **If escalations keep firing on the same question**, that question's answer probably belongs in the knowledge base.

For the underlying mechanics, see [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication) and [Settings → Knowledge Base](/settings/knowledge-base).
