title: "The Daily Shift"
description: "Where to start each shift, what to check, and what to leave to the agents."
---

Most of what happens in Maigrate Hub on any given day happens without you. Candidates apply, get screened, get messaged, book interviews. The shift's actual work is clearing the cases the agents flagged for you, and making sure today's interview pipeline is healthy.

## Suggested cadence

Many teams check Maigrate at the start of each shift and once more after lunch — adjust to your team's volume. The goal is that no escalation sits unresolved for half a day.

## What to do, in order

1. Open Maigrate Hub. The default landing is the [Command Center](/recruiter/command-center).
2. **Work the Escalated Cases list.** These are cases the agents have explicitly flagged for human judgment — ambiguous screening, unclear candidate replies, calendar conflicts, after-hours requests, delivery failures, and repeat no-shows. Click into each one, take the action that matches the type, mark it resolved.
3. **Check the Upcoming Interviews panel** for interviews in the next 48 hours. Spot-check anything that looks off (wrong time, wrong recruiter, candidate not confirmed). Confirm interviews from yesterday were marked **Interview Completed** or **No-Show**.
4. **Skim the Review Queue.** These are candidates the screening agent could not confidently advance or reject — your call. Either accept the `Review` decision, override to `Advance` or `Reject`, or open the [Screening](/recruiter/screening) tab for the full rationale.

<Note>
  **If you only have ten minutes, work the escalations.** The other two panels are the agents running normally — escalations are the cases waiting on you, often with an SLA clock running.
</Note>

## What you don't have to do

- **You don't run follow-ups.** Candidates who don't respond get nudged automatically on the configured cadence. See [Follow-up policy](/recruiter/follow-up-policy) for what they're experiencing.
- **You don't send interview reminders.** A same-day SMS reminder fires automatically.
- **You don't track candidate state in Salesforce.** Maigrate writes back the events that matter — see [Salesforce integration → Writeback events](/integrations/salesforce#writeback-events).

The shift's work is the cases the agents have surfaced. Everything else, the agents handle.
