These pages are recruiter playbooks: short, opinionated walk-throughs of how to use Maigrate Hub through a normal recruiting shift. They focus on the judgment calls — when to step in, when to let the agent finish, what to do when something goes sideways.

For the mechanics — what a button does, what a field means — follow the links into the [Recruiter Guide](/recruiter/command-center) and [Settings](/settings/overview).

## How the agents work, in one paragraph

When a candidate applies, the screening agent reviews their resume and application against the job and your screening directives, then decides whether to advance, review, reject, or escalate. Advanced candidates move to the scheduling agent, which uses Google Calendar to propose slots and books the interview through SMS or email. The communication agent handles replies and follow-ups. Recruiters see all of this in the Command Center and can step in at any point.

## Your four main activities

| Activity | What you use it for |
|---|---|
| [Command Center](/recruiter/command-center) | Daily starting point. Triages unresolved escalations, interviews in the next 48 hours, and applications needing screening review. |
| [Applicants](/recruiter/applicants) | The full application board. Browse, filter, and open any candidate's drawer. |
| [Agent Activity](/recruiter/agent-activity) | Audit trail of recent screening, messaging, interview, and escalation events. |
| [Settings](/settings/overview) | Manage recruiters, Google Workspace, screening directives, scheduling guidance, and templates. |
