## What is Maigrate AI Hub?

Maigrate AI Hub is an AI-powered recruiting workspace. When a new candidate enters the system, agents take over the routine work — screening, outreach, follow-ups, scheduling — and bring your recruiters in whenever they need human judgment.

Think of the agents as junior recruiters: they handle the high-volume, repetitive tasks and know when a situation is beyond their authority. Your senior recruiters stay focused on decisions that actually require them.

## How it works

When a candidate applies, the screening agent reviews them against the job and your screening directives. Advanced candidates move to the scheduling agent, which reads recruiter calendars, proposes slots, and books interviews through SMS or email. The communication agent handles replies and follow-ups. Recruiters see all of this in the [Command Center](/recruiter/command-center) and step in for judgment calls and escalations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Candidate enters the pipeline">
    A new application triggers an intake. Maigrate fetches the candidate's details, resume, job context, and assigned recruiter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Screening agent evaluates fit">
    The AI screening agent reviews the candidate against the job description and your organization's screening directives. It returns `advance`, `review`, or `reject` with a written rationale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scheduling agent sends outreach">
    Advanced candidates receive an interview scheduling message via SMS or email. The agent reads recruiter calendars, proposes concrete time slots, and books confirmed interviews directly to Google Calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Communication agent manages the conversation">
    Inbound replies — confirmations, reschedule requests, questions — are handled automatically. The agent follows up on non-responders and escalates anything ambiguous.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recruiters stay in the loop">
    Escalations, screening reviews, and interview outcomes surface in the Command Center. Recruiters resolve edge cases, override decisions, and send direct messages when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
