When a candidate doesn't reply, Maigrate keeps the conversation alive on a configurable cadence. This page covers the default policy and the same-day interview reminder. Recruiters don't run follow-ups manually — the scheduler does. This is what the candidate is experiencing when a thread looks quiet.

## Default follow-up cadence

The built-in follow-up policy fires up to four attempts, alternating SMS and email, after the initial scheduling outreach has gone unanswered. Each attempt waits for the next moment inside the send window before going out.

| Attempt | Delay since previous send | Preferred channel | Template type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 hours | SMS | Direct CTA |
| 2 | 5 hours | Email | Structured email |
| 3 | 1 business day | SMS | Casual nudge |
| 4 | 2 business days | Email | Soft close |

"Business days" skip weekends. The initial scheduling outreach itself is not counted as an attempt — attempt 1 is the first follow-up after that outreach.

### After the last attempt

After attempt 4 completes without a reply, the thread is marked `unresponsive` with reason `max_attempts_reached`, and the application moves to `closed_unresponsive`. This **does** write back to Salesforce/Lumary: the contact's `lmry__Onboarding_Step__c` flips to `Inactive`. See [Salesforce Integration → Writeback events](/integrations/salesforce#writeback-events).

### Channel rules

| Rule | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Send window | 8 AM – 7 PM in the candidate's local timezone |
| Weekend follow-ups | Disabled unless explicitly enabled in policy |
| Opt-out (`STOP` reply) | SMS removed from this candidate's viable channels; the agent picks the next-best channel or escalates |
| Last channel failed | Policy alternates to the other channel for the next attempt |

You can edit the cadence, send window, and channel preferences in [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication).

## Interview reminders

Once an interview is booked, Maigrate sends up to **two** reminders, each delivered on SMS and email (SMS preferred). All times are in the candidate's local timezone.

| Stage | Target send time |
|---|---|
| Morning-of | 8 AM on the interview day — **except** for interviews before 10 AM local, where it goes out at **3 PM the day before** |
| One hour prior | ~1 hour before the interview start time |

The 10 AM cutoff for the morning-of reminder and the 3 PM day-before fallback are fixed; the 8 AM target shares the `FOLLOW_UP_START_HOUR_LOCAL` bound used by the rest of the follow-up policy. The reminder template is **Interview Reminder** in [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication).

### When a reminder is skipped

A reminder stage is skipped when its computed send time has already passed by the time the send worker evaluates it — for example, if the interview is so close that the one-hour-prior (or morning-of) window has already closed, or the interview itself is already in the past.

<Note>
  Reminders aren't a separate schedule — they live inside the same send-window machinery as the rest of the follow-up policy and are delivered as the hourly send worker fires.
</Note>

## What recruiters see

A thread that looks quiet is usually just waiting for the next attempt to fall inside the send window. Open the [Communication](/recruiter/communication) tab to see the full message history, the agent's reasoning between sends, and the current thread status.

If the candidate is `unresponsive`, the application surfaces in the Applicants board as `closed_unresponsive`. A recruiter can still take over the thread, escalate to another channel manually, or reject the application — see [Communication → Dismissing the agent](/recruiter/communication#dismissing-the-agent).
