title: "Follow-Up Policy"
description: "How Maigrate follows up with candidates who don't respond, and how it reminds them before an interview."
---

When a candidate doesn't reply, Maigrate keeps the conversation alive on a configurable cadence. This page covers the default policy and the same-day interview reminder. Recruiters don't run follow-ups manually — the scheduler does. This is what the candidate is experiencing when a thread looks quiet.

## Default follow-up cadence

The built-in follow-up policy fires up to four attempts, alternating SMS and email, after the initial scheduling outreach has gone unanswered. Each attempt waits for the next moment inside the send window before going out.

| Attempt | Delay since previous send | Preferred channel | Template type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 hours | SMS | Direct CTA |
| 2 | 5 hours | Email | Structured email |
| 3 | 1 business day | SMS | Casual nudge |
| 4 | 2 business days | Email | Soft close |

"Business days" skip weekends. The initial scheduling outreach itself is not counted as an attempt — attempt 1 is the first follow-up after that outreach.

### After the last attempt

After attempt 4 completes without a reply, the thread is marked `unresponsive` with reason `max_attempts_reached`. The application's scheduling status moves to `failed_outreach`. The follow-up policy itself does **not** move the candidate to inactive in Salesforce — that requires a screening decision or a recruiter action. See [Salesforce Integration → Writeback events](/integrations/salesforce#writeback-events).

### Channel rules

| Rule | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Send window | 8 AM – 7 PM in the candidate's local timezone |
| Weekend follow-ups | Disabled unless explicitly enabled in policy |
| Opt-out (`STOP` reply) | SMS removed from this candidate's viable channels; the agent picks the next-best channel or escalates |
| Last channel failed | Policy alternates to the other channel for the next attempt |

You can edit the cadence, send window, and channel preferences in [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication).

## Same-day interview reminder

Once an interview is booked, Maigrate schedules a single SMS reminder for the morning of the interview.

| Behavior | Detail |
|---|---|
| Target send time | 8 AM in the candidate's local timezone, on the interview day |
| Channel | SMS via the configured Twilio number |
| Template | **Same-Day Reminder** in [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication) |

### When the reminder is skipped

| Case | Why |
|---|---|
| Interview is at or before 8 AM candidate-local | No time to fit the reminder before the interview |
| Reminder delivery slips past 7 PM local or past the interview start time | Send window closed |

The 8 AM start and 7 PM cutoff are the same `FOLLOW_UP_START_HOUR_LOCAL` and `FOLLOW_UP_END_HOUR_LOCAL` that bound all follow-ups.

<Note>
  The reminder is queued once the morning of the interview and delivered as soon as the hourly send worker fires after 8 AM local. It is not a configurable separate schedule — it lives inside the same send-window machinery as the rest of the follow-up policy.
</Note>

## What recruiters see

A thread that looks quiet is usually just waiting for the next attempt to fall inside the send window. Open the [Communication](/recruiter/communication) tab to see the full message history, the agent's reasoning between sends, and the current thread status.

If the candidate is `unresponsive`, the application surfaces in the Applicants board with scheduling status `failed_outreach`. A recruiter can take over the thread, escalate to another channel manually, or reject the application — see [Communication → Dismissing the agent](/recruiter/communication#dismissing-the-agent).
