---
title: "Scheduling"
description: "How Maigrate schedules interviews — from initial outreach through booking, reminders, and no-shows."
---

Once a candidate is advanced, the scheduling agent takes over. It reads recruiter calendars, proposes interview slots, handles the candidate's reply, and books the Google Calendar event.

## How scheduling starts

Scheduling can be triggered two ways:

1. **Automatically** — when `AUTO_SCHEDULE_ON_ADVANCE` is enabled, advancing a candidate (by the agent or by recruiter override) queues scheduling immediately
2. **Manually** — click **Schedule Interview** in the Details tab of the application drawer

When triggered, the scheduling agent:
1. Assigns a recruiter if one isn't already assigned
2. Reads the recruiter's Google Calendar for the next 7 days
3. Generates up to 4 available interview slots (30-minute blocks within business hours)
4. Creates a communication thread and queues the communication agent to send the first outreach

## The initial outreach

The first message to the candidate proposes the available slots (or a booking link, if one is configured). The message is sent via SMS if the candidate has a valid mobile number, or email if not.

After the outreach is sent, the application status moves to `Awaiting candidate response`.

## What happens when the candidate replies

The communication agent handles inbound replies:

| Reply type | Agent action |
|---|---|
| Selects a slot | Books the interview on Google Calendar, sends a confirmation, status → `Scheduled` |
| Asks for different times | Searches for new availability and responds with options |
| Asks a question | Answers using the knowledge base, stays in scheduling mode |
| Weekend/after-hours request | Escalates to a human (policy: don't force daytime alternatives unilaterally) |
| Ambiguous or concerning reply | Escalates to a human |
| No reply | Follow-up automation kicks in (see below) |

## Follow-up automation

If a candidate doesn't respond, Maigrate follows up automatically based on the follow-up policy configured in [Settings → Scheduling](/settings/scheduling) and [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication). The timing, number of attempts, and which channel each follow-up uses are all configurable.

All sends respect a quiet-hours window in the candidate's local timezone. After the maximum attempts are exhausted, the thread is marked `unresponsive` and status becomes `Failed outreach`.

## Booking

The scheduling agent books the interview only after the candidate confirms a slot. Booking:
- Creates or updates a Google Calendar event on the recruiter's calendar
- Requests a Google Meet link automatically
- Sends calendar invites to recruiter and candidate
- Records the interview in Maigrate

## Interview reminders

Maigrate sends automatic reminders before confirmed interviews:

| Interview time | When reminder sends |
|---|---|
| Before 11 AM candidate-local | 3 PM the previous day |
| After 3 PM candidate-local | 3 PM the same day |
| 11 AM–3 PM | No reminder sent |

## No-shows and rescheduling

After an interview is booked, the recruiter marks the outcome from the Command Center or Details tab.

**Mark No-Show**: triggers the scheduling agent to reach out and reschedule. The candidate receives a new slot offer.

**Repeat no-show**: a second no-show creates an escalation in the Command Center with a reject recommendation. The recruiter decides whether to reject or give the candidate another chance.

**Mark Completed**: closes the scheduling workflow.

## Scheduling settings

You can configure slot duration, recruiter business hours, follow-up timing, and scheduling guidance in [Settings → Scheduling](/settings/scheduling).
