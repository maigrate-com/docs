title: "Interview Agenda"
description: "A day-by-day view of every interview and how it turned out — completed, no-show, canceled, or still pending."
---

The Interview Agenda answers one question at a glance: **for a given day, what happened with each interview?** Unlike the upcoming-interviews feed, the agenda shows every interview booked for the day you pick — not just the ones still marked as scheduled — so outcomes never slip through the cracks.

## Choosing a day

The agenda opens on **today** by default. Use the date picker, or the **‹ Prev**, **Today**, and **Next ›** shortcuts, to move to any day — past or future.

- **Past days** show what actually happened: which interviews were completed, which were no-shows, and which were canceled.
- **Today** shows a "now" line that separates interviews that have already passed from the ones still ahead.
- **Future days** show what's booked and confirmed.

You can also filter by recruiter to see just one person's day, or leave it on **All recruiters**.

## Reading a row

Each interview on the agenda shows, at a glance:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Time | When the interview is scheduled, in order down the day |
| Candidate | Name and email |
| Recruiter | Who's running the interview (color-coded) |
| Status | Confirmed, Completed, No-Show, Canceled, or Rescheduled |
| Reminder | Whether the candidate's reminder was Sent, is still Scheduled, or Failed — hover to see the exact time |
| Last message | The most recent message exchanged with the candidate, with how long ago it was |

A summary line at the top totals the day: how many interviews are completed, no-shows, canceled, pending, and still awaiting an outcome.

## Marking an outcome

You can record what happened without leaving the agenda. On any scheduled interview, open the menu next to the status and choose:

- **Mark Completed** — one click, for interviews that happened.
- **Mark No-Show…** — opens a dialog where you can add an optional private note for the scheduling agent. Once marked, the agent automatically reaches out to reschedule.
- **Open Google Meet** or **Open Calendar event** — jump straight to the video call or the calendar entry.

Marking an outcome here does exactly the same thing as marking it from the application drawer — use whichever is faster in the moment. Recording outcomes promptly is what keeps the agent moving; see [Scheduling](/recruiter/scheduling#recording-the-interview-outcome) for why it matters.

<Note>
  To **cancel** or **reschedule** an interview, open the candidate's application drawer — those actions still live there. The agenda is for seeing the day and recording outcomes.
</Note>
