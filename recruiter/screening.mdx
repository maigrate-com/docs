title: "Screening"
description: "How the AI screening agent evaluates candidates, what its decisions mean, and how to override them."
---

Screening is the first automated step after a candidate enters from Salesforce. The screening agent evaluates the candidate against the job and returns a decision with a written rationale.

## Decision types

| Decision | What it means | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **Advance** | Candidate meets the criteria for an interview | Scheduling starts automatically (if auto-schedule is enabled) |
| **Review** | Agent is uncertain — human judgment needed | Candidate appears in the Review Queue; no automatic scheduling |
| **Reject** | Candidate does not meet criteria | Scheduling status set to rejected; writeback sent to Salesforce |

<Note>
  There is a fourth internal state: `escalate_to_human`. This is stored as `Review` in the UI and creates an escalation in the Command Center. You'll see it in the escalation queue with the agent's reason.
</Note>

## What the agent evaluates

The screening agent considers:

1. **Job description** — title, full description, location, requirements from Workable
2. **Application answers** — the candidate's own responses to application questions
3. **Candidate experience summary** — education and experience text from Salesforce
4. **Screening directives** — your organization's custom criteria configured in [Settings → Screening](/settings/screening)

The agent is instructed not to invent facts. When evidence is ambiguous or missing, it prefers `Review` over `Reject`.

## Viewing a screening decision

Open any application and click the **Screening** tab. You'll see:

- The **decision badge** — color-coded `Advance`, `Review`, or `Reject`
- **Rationale** — the agent's written explanation of why it made this decision, specific to the candidate and role
- **Candidate evidence** — a structured view of the application data the agent used (answers, experience, education)
- **Job context** — the job description the agent evaluated against

## Overriding a decision

Recruiters can override any screening decision.

1. In the Screening tab, click the **Override** button or select from the decision dropdown
2. Choose the correct decision
3. Enter a reason (required — this is stored in the audit log and written back to Salesforce)
4. Click **Save Override**

Your override replaces the agent's decision as the authoritative outcome. If you override to `Advance`, you can then manually trigger scheduling from the Details tab.

<Warning>
  Overriding to `Reject` writes back to Salesforce immediately. A rejection note is created on the Salesforce Contact with your reason.
</Warning>

## Auto-screening and manual trigger

Screening runs automatically after intake by default. If it didn't run (or you want to re-run it after updating screening directives), use the **Run Screening** button in the Details tab.

## Screening directives

You configure the criteria the agent uses in [Settings → Screening](/settings/screening). Directives are organization-wide instructions that supplement the job description — for example, minimum experience requirements or disqualifying factors specific to your organization.
