The Command Center is the default landing page in Maigrate AI Hub. It surfaces the highest-priority items across your recruiting pipeline so you know exactly what needs attention right now.

## The three panels

### Escalations

Escalations are first-class records created when an agent encounters a situation it cannot resolve on its own. They block further automation on the affected candidate until a recruiter acts.

The escalation badge in the sidebar shows the total count of unresolved escalations. The Command Center lists them in priority order — oldest and most time-sensitive first.

Each escalation card shows:
- **Candidate name and role** — who it's about
- **Reason** — a short label (e.g., "Ambiguous reply", "Calendar conflict", "Repeat no-show")
- **Source agent** — which agent created it
- **Age** — how long it's been waiting

Click an escalation to open the candidate's full detail drawer and resolve it. See [Escalations](/recruiter/escalations) for the full resolution workflow.

### Upcoming Interviews

A list of interviews scheduled in the next 48 hours. This panel helps you stay prepared and catch any last-minute issues.

For each interview you can see:
- Candidate name and recruiter assigned
- Interview time (in your local timezone)
- A Join Meeting link and a calendar event link

After each interview is over, open the application from the Upcoming Interviews panel (or from Applicants) and record the outcome — **Interview Completed** or **No-Show** — on the Details tab. See [Scheduling — Recording the interview outcome](/recruiter/scheduling#recording-the-interview-outcome) for the full SOP.

### Review Queue

Applications with a screening decision of `Review` — meaning the agent flagged them for human judgment rather than advancing or rejecting automatically.

These are not blocking the pipeline, but they won't move to scheduling until a recruiter either:
- Accepts the `Review` decision (and manually triggers scheduling if appropriate), or
- Overrides it to `Advance` (which can trigger automatic scheduling) or `Reject`

See [Screening](/recruiter/screening) for how to work with screening decisions and overrides.

## Sidebar badge

The escalation count badge on the Command Center nav item updates in real time. It resets to zero when all escalations are resolved.

## What to check first

A healthy daily routine:
1. Clear the escalation queue — unresolved escalations can delay candidates and miss SLAs
2. Confirm any interviews from the last 24 hours as completed or no-show
3. Skim the review queue for any candidates you want to advance manually

<Note>
  **If you only have ten minutes, work the escalations.** The other two panels are the agents running normally — escalations are the cases waiting on you.
</Note>
