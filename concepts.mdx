Understanding these terms will make the rest of the docs — and the app itself — much easier to follow.

## Core entities

### Candidate

The person applying for a job. Candidates enter Maigrate from Salesforce (as a `Contact` record). Maigrate stores a local copy for operational purposes, but Salesforce remains the source of truth for identity and contact data.

### Application

A candidate's application to a specific role. The application is the core workflow object — it carries the screening decision, scheduling status, recruiter assignment, and links to the communication thread.

A candidate can have multiple applications (one per role).

### Job

The role being recruited for. Job metadata (title, description, location, requirements) is cached from Workable via Salesforce. The screening agent uses it to evaluate candidate fit.

### Recruiter

A human team member in Maigrate. Recruiters own the candidate relationship: they review screening decisions, manage availability, mark no-shows, and handle escalations. Each recruiter has a connected Google Calendar used for interview scheduling.

## Agents

Maigrate has three AI agents, each responsible for a specific workflow domain.

### Screening Agent

Reads the candidate profile, job description, application answers, and organization-level screening directives. Returns a decision (`advance`, `review`, or `reject`) with a written rationale.

The recruiter can override any screening decision from the Screening tab.

### Scheduling Agent

Manages the full interview scheduling lifecycle: reading recruiter calendar availability, proposing time slots, sending the initial scheduling outreach, handling slot selection, booking the Google Calendar event, and managing reschedules and no-shows.

### Communication Agent

Writes and sends candidate-facing messages (SMS, email). Handles inbound replies, evaluates whether the candidate's response is actionable or ambiguous, and decides next steps. It also runs follow-up automation when candidates don't respond.

## Workflow concepts

### Directive

The brief given to the communication or scheduling agent for a specific thread. It specifies the goal ("schedule an interview"), allowed channels, tone, constraints, and context. Directives are created automatically by the system and can be influenced by operator guidance.

### Thread

The ongoing conversation with a candidate. One thread per workflow interaction (e.g., one thread for initial scheduling outreach). The thread stores the full message history, current status, follow-up state, and the active directive.

### Escalation

A first-class record created when an agent needs a human to step in. Escalations surface in the Command Center and block further automation on the affected application until resolved.

Common causes:
- Ambiguous or concerning candidate reply
- Scheduling conflicts that can't be resolved automatically
- Repeated no-shows
- Document validation failures
- Weekend/after-hours exception requests

Each escalation includes the agent's explanation of what happened and recommendations for resolution.

### Operator Guidance

A private note a recruiter writes to an agent, visible in the Communication tab. The agent treats it as authoritative instruction but never shows it verbatim to the candidate. Use it to course-correct the agent ("Skip the next follow-up — I'll call them directly") or provide context it doesn't have.

## Communication concepts

### Channel routing

Maigrate chooses the outreach channel automatically:

| Condition | Channel |
|---|---|
| Valid mobile phone | SMS via Twilio |
| Invalid phone or landline | Email via Resend |
| No phone | Email (or escalation if email is also missing) |

Candidates can opt out of SMS; those opt-outs are stored and respected on future sends.

### Follow-up policy

When a candidate doesn't respond to a scheduling message, the follow-up policy kicks in automatically:

| Attempt | Timing | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First available send window | SMS |
| 2 | 5 hours later | Email |
| 3 | 1 day later | SMS |
| 4 | 2 days later | Email |

After 4 unanswered attempts, the thread is marked `unresponsive`. All sends respect a window of 8 AM–9 PM in the candidate's local timezone.

## Salesforce integration

### Writeback

When key events happen in Maigrate, they are written back to the Salesforce Contact record. This keeps Salesforce/Lumary accurate without manual data entry.

Events that trigger a writeback: candidate advanced, interview scheduled, no-show, candidate withdrew, candidate rejected, candidate-facing message sent.

### Reconciliation

A background job runs every 6 hours to pull in Salesforce Contacts that may have been missed by the real-time push. This is a safety net, not the primary intake path.
