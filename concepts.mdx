Understanding these terms will make the rest of the docs — and the app itself — much easier to follow.

## Core entities

### Candidate

The person applying for a job. Maigrate stores a working copy of the candidate's contact details and application data for use across the automated workflow.

### Application

A candidate's application to a specific role. The application is the core workflow object — it carries the screening decision, scheduling status, recruiter assignment, and links to the communication thread.

A candidate can have multiple applications (one per role).

### Job

The role being recruited for. Job metadata (title, description, location, requirements) is pulled from your ATS and used by the screening agent to evaluate candidate fit.

### Recruiter

A human team member in Maigrate. Recruiters own the candidate relationship: they review screening decisions, manage availability, mark no-shows, and handle escalations. Each recruiter has a connected Google Calendar used for interview scheduling.

## Agents

Maigrate has three AI agents. Think of them as junior recruiters who handle the high-volume work and bring issues to a senior recruiter (you) whenever the situation needs human judgment.

### Screening Agent

Reviews each application — the candidate profile, job description, application answers, and your screening directives — and returns a recommendation (`advance`, `review`, or `reject`) with a written rationale. Like a junior screener giving you a summary and recommendation before you decide.

You can override any screening decision from the Screening tab.

### Scheduling Agent

Handles the calendar coordination: reads recruiter availability, proposes interview times, sends the outreach, handles the candidate's reply, and books the Google Calendar event. It also manages reschedules and chases non-responders. When it hits a situation it can't resolve (conflicting constraints, repeated no-shows, special requests), it escalates to you.

### Communication Agent

Manages the conversation with the candidate — writing messages, reading replies, deciding next steps. It knows when a reply is clear enough to act on and when something is ambiguous enough to need a human. It runs follow-up messages automatically when candidates go quiet, and escalates when the conversation goes somewhere it can't handle alone.

## Workflow concepts

### Directive

The brief given to the communication or scheduling agent for a specific thread. It specifies the goal ("schedule an interview"), allowed channels, tone, constraints, and context. Directives are created automatically by the system and can be influenced by operator guidance.

### Thread

The ongoing conversation with a candidate. One thread per workflow interaction (e.g., one thread for initial scheduling outreach). The thread stores the full message history, current status, follow-up state, and the active directive.

### Escalation

A first-class record created when an agent needs a human to step in. Escalations surface in the Command Center and block further automation on the affected application until resolved.

Common causes:
- Ambiguous or concerning candidate reply
- Scheduling conflicts that can't be resolved automatically
- Repeated no-shows
- Document validation failures
- Weekend/after-hours exception requests

Each escalation includes the agent's explanation of what happened and recommendations for resolution.

### Operator Guidance

A private note a recruiter writes to an agent, visible in the Communication tab. The agent treats it as authoritative instruction but never shows it verbatim to the candidate. Use it to course-correct the agent ("Skip the next follow-up — I'll call them directly") or provide context it doesn't have.

## Communication concepts

### Channel routing

Maigrate chooses the outreach channel automatically:

| Condition | Channel |
|---|---|
| Valid mobile phone | SMS via Twilio |
| Invalid phone or landline | Email via Resend |
| No phone | Email (or escalation if email is also missing) |

Candidates can opt out of SMS; those opt-outs are stored and respected on future sends.

### Follow-up policy

When a candidate doesn't respond to a scheduling message, the follow-up policy kicks in automatically. The timing, number of attempts, and channels are configurable in [Settings → Scheduling](/settings/scheduling) and [Settings → Communication](/settings/communication). All sends respect a quiet-hours window so candidates aren't messaged late at night.

After the configured maximum attempts are exhausted, the thread is marked `unresponsive` and no further automated messages are sent.
